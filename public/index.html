La Storia

A small room, made for slowness.

NERO began as one espresso machine and a folding table. We've since added a wood-fired oven and a short kitchen menu, but the rule hasn't changed: do one thing properly, in a room with nowhere to rush to.

Our beans come from three estates at a time, roasted every Tuesday and rested before they reach the grinder. Pastry starts at five each morning.
